## Mars Trail

`mars-trail/index.html` is a browser game in the spirit of *The Oregon Trail*, but the trail goes to Mars. Open the file in any modern browser. There is no build step and nothing to install.

1. **Outfit**: choose a leader (tech billionaire, flight pilot, scientist or plumber), hire up to five specialists (chef, medic, engineer, race car driver, computer nerd, carpenter, botanist, pro gamer), pick a ship and stock the hold. A laser turret is optional.
2. **Launch control**: read the weather board, call GO or SCRUB, then hit LAUNCH at T-0.
3. **Ascent**: dodge satellites and debris on the way out of low Earth orbit.
4. **Cockpit cruise**: fly from the cockpit with a throttle lever, a nav stick that fights drift, warning lights and a warp-speed window. Along the way: a course correction, docking with the Aldrin Cycler trading station, an electrical fault to rewire, an alien message to decode, a meteor storm and plenty of random events.
5. **Landing**: practice in the simulator in Mars orbit, then fly the lander onto the pad.
6. **Base**: build habitats, solar arrays, greenhouses, ice extractors and oxygenators, drive the rover and scrub panels in dust storms. Survive 150 sols and get the base ready for the 12 colonists of the second wave.
