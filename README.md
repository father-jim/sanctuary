## Mars Trail

`mars-trail/index.html` is a browser game in the spirit of *The Oregon Trail*, but the trail goes to Mars. Open the file in any modern browser. There is no build step and nothing to install.

1. **Outfit**: pick a background (budget and score multiplier), name your crew, choose a ship and stock the hold.
2. **Launch control**: read the weather board, call GO or SCRUB, then hit LAUNCH at T-0.
3. **Ascent**: dodge satellites and debris on the way out of low Earth orbit.
4. **Cruise**: manage pace, rations, repairs and medicine through random events, course corrections and a strange radio signal.
5. **Landing**: fly the lander onto the pad by hand.
6. **Base**: build habitats, solar arrays, greenhouses, ice extractors and oxygenators, then survive 150 sols until the second wave arrives.
